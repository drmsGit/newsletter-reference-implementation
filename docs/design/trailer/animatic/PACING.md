# Animatic: pacing report

The first animatic of the trailer: the reference stills in storyboard order, a **synthetic read of script A** (macOS voice Daniel, en_GB), a full-length mood score (no strings, no piano) and captions. It tests the pacing; it is not the trailer and it does not use the frames the film will have. See [[TRAILER-BRIEF]] and [[STORYBOARD]]. Open `animatic.html` in Chrome and press Play.

**Speech:** 186 s of 286 s, 65 percent of the film is narrated. The read is as slow as the voice goes; pauses of 0.45 s follow each sentence (0.16 s in the S18 list) and 0.13 s each comma.

**Tight scenes** (under one second of headroom): S05 (0.1 s), S18 (0.3 s), S19 (0.6 s). 
A slower human read will overflow these, so either the scene gets a second or two, or the line is trimmed. Scenes with a lot of room (over eight seconds of headroom): S20 (11.6 s). S08 shows three shots over many seconds with a short line, and the end card holds its silence by design.

**What the tracks are.** `mix.m4a` is narration over the score with the music ducked while the voice speaks; `narration.m4a` and `music.m4a` are the two layers alone. `captions.srt` is cut from the narration and timed over the measured audio. The score is arranged on the scene timeline: a pulse and three hits in the hook, a rising pad under the problem, mood A for the manager, mood B and one low hit per file drop for the developer, a swell that cuts to silence at the three lines, one soft mallet note on the end card.

**Not in the animatic:** the redrawn frames, the camera moves, the sound of clicks tied to the cursor, the first-person script B.
