# Trailer storyboard

Twenty-two scenes, about 4:46. Read [[TRAILER-BRIEF]] first: it holds the decisions, the look, the claim ledger and the checks that must be done before any frame is built. [[VOICEOVER]] has both narration scripts clean for recording. `storyboard.html` shows these scenes as cards with their reference frames.

**How to read a scene.** *See* is the shot, in order. *Source* is where the frame comes from, as a path under `docs/design/`. **Live** is a capture in `ux-audit-2026-10-17/screenshots/` (brand Grathaus Outdoor, real demo data, German email content). **Render** is a drawn board in `renders/` (brand Nordlicht Kaffee, invented data: layout only). *Text* is burned-in on screen. *A* and *B* are the narration (third person, first person). *Evidence* is the ADR or code that makes the scene true. *Check* is what has to be confirmed before the frame is built. **T** marks a scene that is in the 60 to 75 second teaser.

All UI frames are drawn at 1440×810 logical px and rendered at 3840×2160. Where a live capture and a render disagree, the live capture wins.

**Reference images are not the source.** Every "Live" image below is an audit capture of 2026-10-17, 16:54, and many UI fixes have landed since (for example the create dialogs, which the capture shows with the title beside its field). A frame is drawn from the running app at a recorded commit, never from a capture alone: [[TRAILER-BRIEF]], section 8, "Which version of the app the frames draw".

**Corrected 2026-10-17, after the second round of checks ([[PREPRODUCTION-CHECKS-2]]) and the evening handoff ([[HANDOFF-2026-10-17]]):** S03b, S05, S06, S08, S16, S16b, S17 and S18 carry what was verified on a scratch copy; every email frame changes, because the demo brands' profiles now give their emails a look (Grathaus Outdoor's wear the platform palette, Grathaus Touren's an alpine look), so email frames are captured again; every frame with the navigation carries a "Keyboard shortcuts" button.

**Corrected 2026-10-17, after the first pre-production checks** ([[PREPRODUCTION-CHECKS]]): S01, S06 and S07 to S16 carry what was verified on a scratch copy of the demo. The narration of S07, S09 and S10 changed (see [[VOICEOVER]]), and three of the corrections are Claude's choices that Diede has not reviewed ([[TRAILER-BRIEF]], end of section 16). That the film says nothing about unsubscribing is Diede's decision (section 16, decision 8), applied to S09 to S11 and S13.
