## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
📋 **Sales and transaction data from the CRM or DWH — probably a playbook chapter rather than an implementation.** Surfaced 2026-09-18 (user). Getting revenue and order history into the platform and personalising on it is **too company-specific to ship**: *"this is very different from business to business, so this may be not implemented"*. The deliverable asked for instead is a **worked extension chapter**: *"you can create tables like this and add py files like this and now you not just have a product catalog, but connected to the recipient their sales data. if you want to use it in an email -> this is how you can adjust your modules"*. That makes it a **Phase 4D artefact** (the "AI-open" / evolve-the-package story) and a direct test of whether the plugin seams — decision strategies, modules, channels, renderers, AI tasks — actually compose for something the architecture never anticipated. **If the chapter cannot be written against the real seams, the seams are the finding**, and that is worth more than the chapter.
