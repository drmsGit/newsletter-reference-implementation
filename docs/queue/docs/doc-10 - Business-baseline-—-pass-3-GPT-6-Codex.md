## Method and limits

Coverage includes the ADR directory inventory; every Decision/addendum paragraph's topic sentence and every numbered rule, with full relevant sections read for candidate themes; headings/questions/resolutions throughout the entire interview-prep tree, including design and final-design records; both business interview files; playbook §5; all business-file inventories and relevant sections; open tasks through the Backlog CLI plus corpus searches of open and completed tasks and detailed completed-task reads for changed premises. Architecture Code pages were consulted before corresponding source checks. Source citations, not page descriptions alone, support the changed P2 conclusions.

This was an index-driven semantic sweep and targeted source verification, **not a verbatim reread of every essay, every task or every code file**. The entire task-description corpus, all non-Decision ADR boilerplate, every frontend implementation, and every competitive-market claim were not exhaustively revalidated. No live legal or market assessment was attempted. No environment file, live database or mailbox was read; no delivery was triggered; no tests or mutations were run. No fixes are proposed. Final snapshot advanced to HEAD 05088de4 during the shared review; delivery-source line changes in that update do not affect the cited auth/consent/audit/AI premise checks above.
