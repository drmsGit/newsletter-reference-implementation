## Three items the caller asked about specifically

1. **ADR-161's two addenda on point 7 (2026-09-24, 2026-09-27) do not
   contradict each other.** The first addendum falsifies the
   catalogue-readiness rider and explicitly calls itself "a contradiction
   rather than a narrowing." The second addendum narrows the
   `module manifest = fields and limits` clause (moving field *definitions*
   to `storage/fields.json`, per ADR-177) and explicitly self-labels as "a
   narrowing, not a contradiction — unlike the 2026-09-24 addendum above."
   It quotes the first addendum's closing sentence accurately before
   qualifying it. Reads as a clean, well-labeled sequence.

2. **ADR-176 amending itself 3 days after its own acceptance (2026-09-24 →
   addendum 2026-09-27) is a correct use of the addendum instrument, not a
   misuse.** The addendum falsifies one clause of point 5 (removes the
   `channel` parameter's default) while leaving the rest of point 5 and all
   other points in force — the same shape as ADR-161's first addendum
   (falsify one clause via addendum, no supersession). Nothing was built
   against the original default (points 1 and 4 of ADR-176 are themselves
   still "accepted and not implemented" per the addendum's own verification),
   so there was no shipped behavior to contradict. The only genuinely unusual
   thing is the compressed timeline, not the mechanism — this is a process
   oddity worth watching for recurrence, not a drift finding.
